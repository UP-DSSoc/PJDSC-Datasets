# ❕ Introduction  
This repository contains the official datasets, reference materials, and selected source codes for the Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge (PJDSC), the flagship annual competition organized by the UP Data Science Society (UP DSSoc) since 2021.

As part of UP DSSoc’s commitment to making data science education more accessible and open, this repository serves as a centralized archive of competition materials across different PJDSC editions. It includes *datasets released to participants, final round submissions, and other relevant resources* intended to promote transparency and continued learning in data science.

---

## About Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge (PJDSC)

The Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge (PJDSC) by UP Data Science Society (UP DSSoc) is a nationwide data science event open to senior high school students, undergraduate students, and recent graduates (see eligibility below). Each edition consists of **talks, workshops, and a data science project proposal competition**, all of which aim to provide an avenue for students to learn, apply, and develop data skills in a competitive setting.

## 🙋🏽 About the Organizers

Founded in 2020, UP Data Science Society (UP DSSoc) is the first data science organization in University of the Philippines (UP). The organization aims to provide an environment for aspiring data scientists to engage in insightful data science discussions and to hone their skills, regardless of skill level, through workshops and other opportunities. UP DSSoc also envisions itself as a multidisciplinary organization that equips and empowers members to use data science to produce data-driven solutions for social good, aligning both internal and external events to contribute to the advancement of communities at large. The work and activities held in UP DSSoc revolve around the organization core values of Purposeful Learning, Community for Innovation, and Utak at Puso.

### OUR VISION
A multidisciplinary organization whose members contribute to society through innovative solutions to community problems using Data Science, and help responsibly develop the field of Data Science in the country.

### OUR MISSION
To provide an avenue for Data Science enthusiasts of UP Diliman to safely share ideas and experiences, hone their skills, and collaborate to build socially relevant and impactful projects while promoting data science and its real life applications.

# ❔ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. I am not from Computer Science, Statistics, Engineering, or any course directly related to Data Science. Am I still allowed to register?  
    - The competition is open to all undergraduates from any university with no restrictions on degree programs.

2. I’m an undergraduate but currently on LOA, can I still participate?  
   - Yes, you may still participate if you are an undergraduate who is currently on Leave of Absence.

3. Is this open to graduate students?  
   - Yes, provided that no more than 2 years have passed after you graduated from your undergraduate studies.

4. Do I need to have any programming experience to join the competition?  
   - It is not a requirement, but it would help. We will hold a workshop with learning sessions to be able to widen your knowledge in data science but most of the programming aspect is left for you to learn.

5. What programming language are we allowed to use?  
   - You are allowed to use anything as long as you are able to successfully formulate your proposed data science solutions.

6. Can our team have members from different universities and regions?  
   - Yes. You are free to team up with members from all over the Philippines as long as they meet the criteria for joining.

7. Where will the Hybrid/Face-to-face events take place?  
   - The venue for the hybrid/face-to-face events are to be announced in the Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge Facebook page.

8. What if one of our team members drops out during the event?  
   - It is okay as long as you still have at least 3 members. However, if you do not meet the minimum number of members, you will need to find a replacement member.

9. Where will the hybrid or face-to-face events be held?  
   - The venue will be announced at a later date. Please refer to our official Facebook page for the latest updates.

10. Can non-finalist participants attend the final round or culminating event?  
    - Depending on the attendance limit of the culminating event, non-finalist participants are welcome to attend the final culminating event as visitors. They may explore the booths of finalist teams, as well as those of event sponsors and partners for networking and engagement opportunities.
