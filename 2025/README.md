# Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge 2025  
### Organized by the UP Data Science Society  

`🕒 Last Updated: February 2026`

---

## 🚀 Theme  
**Weathering the Storm: Uncovering Climate Solutions for Atmospheric Resilience**  

The Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge 2025 is a national competition organized by the University of the Philippines Data Science Society (UPDSSoc). This year’s PJDSC challenges participants to leverage data science to analyze complex climate and weather data and develop smarter early warning systems, climate adaptation plans, and evidence-based solutions to improve atmospheric resilience across Filipino communities.

## 🌍 Purpose of the Competition

The challenge was designed to:  
- Promote awareness of **atmospheric resilience and climate solutions** through data.  
- Equip students with **practical data science skills**.  
- Inspire **innovative solutions** to complex atmospheric and climate problems.  
- Foster a **collaborative community** of young data scientists nationwide.
