## 1. Project CCHAIN Datasets
The Project Climate Change, Health, and Artificial Intelligence (Project CCHAIN) dataset is a validated, open-source linked dataset covering 20 years (2003–2022) of climate, environmental, socioeconomic, and health dimensions at the barangay (village) level across 12 Philippine cities (Dagupan, Palayan, Navotas, Mandaluyong, Muntinlupa, Legazpi, Iloilo, Mandaue, Tacloban, Zamboanga, Cagayan de Oro, Davao).  

- **Source:** [Project CCHAIN, Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thinkdatasci/project-cchain/)  
- **Website:** [Project CCHAIN](https://thinkingmachines.github.io/project-cchain/)  
- **Request Dataset:** [Data Request](https://thinkingmachines.github.io/project-cchain/)

---

## 2. PAGASA Climate Information Map (CliMap)
High-resolution climate projections and extremes indices for rainfall and temperature across Philippine provinces, including heatwave and temperature anomaly data.  

- **Source:** [PAGASA CliMap](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/climate-change/dynamic-downscaling/climap)  
- **Request Dataset:** [CliMap Download](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/climate-change/dynamic-downscaling/climap) (Click province → download CLIRAM/CERAM tables → fill form → receive Excel/CSV via email)

---

## 3. Philippine Climate Change Indicators Dashboard (IMF)
Aggregated indicators for climate change impacts and adaptation, including emissions, energy use, and resilience metrics.  

- **Source:** [IMF Climate Data](https://climatedata.imf.org/pages/access-data)  
- **Dataset:** [IMF Dataset](https://climatedata.imf.org/datasets/29653214f9424adc99aa2b4dc9049926/about)

---

## 4. Climate Change Knowledge Portal — Sea Level Rise Projections
Coastal exposure data showing projected sea level rise and inundation risks.  

- **Source:** [World Bank Climate Knowledge Portal](https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org/country/philippines/sea-level-historical)  
- **Dataset:** [Sea Level Data](https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org/download-data#htab-1503)

---

## 5. Philippine Tropical Cyclone Tracks (GeoJSON)
Historical tracks of tropical cyclones entering the Philippine Area of Responsibility, compiled from PAGASA and humanitarian sources.  

- **Sources:**  
  - [PAGASA Annual Tropical Cyclone Tracks](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/information/annual-cyclone-track)  
  - [PDRF](https://www.pdrf.org/)  
  - [Humdata Organization](https://data.humdata.org/organization/c9acf432-a02a-4342-ba62-d22acca0af97)  

- **Datasets:**  
  - [2024 Tropical Cyclone Tracks](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/02da0e67-e9d9-496a-bfdf-f656320ce2f2)  
  - [Tropical Cyclone Carina (Gaemi) Private Sector Response Efforts](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/tropical-cyclone-carina-gaemi-private-sector-response-efforts)  
  - [Tropical Cyclone Kristine (Trami) Private Sector Response Efforts](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippines-tropical-cyclone-kristine-trami-private-sector-response-efforts)  
  - [Tropical Cyclone Nika, Ofel, and Pepito Private Sector Response Efforts](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippines-tropical-cyclone-nika-ofel-and-pepito-private-sector-response-efforts)
